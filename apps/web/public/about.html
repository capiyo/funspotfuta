About Funspot

Funspot is a football fan community built around match discussions, football channels, fan posts, match predictions and community leaderboards. It brings football conversations together around teams, competitions and fixtures.

Football conversations

Use channels and fixture discussions to follow topics that interest you, share opinions about matches and take part in community conversations.

Community participation

Funspot includes fan posts, predictions and leaderboards so supporters can participate and explore community activity. Interactive features may require an account.
