Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains how Funspot handles information when you use the Funspot football-fan community website.

Information and account activity

When you create an account or use community features, Funspot may process information you submit, such as account details, profile information, posts, comments, votes, and messages, to provide and secure those features. Do not post information you do not want other users to see in public community areas.

Cookies and similar technologies

Funspot and service providers may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to keep the website working, remember preferences, protect accounts, measure performance, and support advertising. Browser settings can be used to control or delete cookies and stored data, although some features may then work differently.

Google advertising

Funspot may use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize ads where permitted by law and your consent choices. Learn how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services at Google’s partner-site privacy information. You can manage ad personalization through Google My Ad Center.

Where legally required, advertising and non-essential storage technologies should be enabled only after the appropriate consent has been obtained. The availability and personalization of ads may vary by region and user settings.

Service providers and sharing

Funspot may rely on hosting, authentication, infrastructure, analytics, and advertising providers to operate the website. Those providers may process information as needed to provide their services, subject to their terms and applicable law. Funspot does not sell account passwords.

Retention and security

Information is retained for as long as reasonably needed to provide features, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and contact

You can manage browser cookies and Google ad personalization using the controls linked above. For account-data requests or privacy questions, contact the Funspot service operator through the contact method provided in the app or on the official Funspot website.

This policy should be reviewed and updated by the service operator to reflect the deployed data practices, applicable jurisdictions, and any consent-management platform used by the live website.
