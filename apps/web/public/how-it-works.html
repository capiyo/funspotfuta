How Funspot Works

Funspot organizes football community activity around channels and fixtures, helping supporters find discussions about the teams and competitions they follow.

Follow football channels

Browse channels to find communities and conversations focused on football competitions and topics.

Discuss matches

Use fixture-related chats and community posts to share opinions and talk about teams and upcoming matches.

Predictions and leaderboards

Funspot supports community match predictions and leaderboards. Predictions are opinions about sporting outcomes, not guarantees.

Pokes use points, not cash stakes

In the current app implementation, a Poke reserves points from a user's points balance. Another user can fill an open Poke by choosing the opposing side; the feature is implemented separately from money bets and payment flows. Poke points are in-app points, and this description does not promise cash prizes or withdrawals. Check the app's current rules before participating.

Accounts and privacy

Some interactive features require sign-in. Review the Privacy Policy to understand how information may be handled.
