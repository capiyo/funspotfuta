A home for the football conversation

Funspot is a football fan community built around the moments supporters care about: the next fixture, the big team debate, the prediction before kick-off and the conversation that carries on after the final whistle. Follow the football you care about, meet fans who see the game differently and make your match opinion part of the community picture.

Football, organized around the match

Funspot connects fan activity to channels and fixtures. A channel gives supporters a place to gather around a competition or football subject. A fixture brings the conversation into focus around a particular match, so fans can follow the build-up, exchange views and return to the same match context as events unfold.

Find your football people

Explore channels built around competitions and shared interests. Join conversations with supporters who follow the same teams, leagues and football stories.

Keep the match in view

Fixture discussions keep team context close to the conversation. Follow the match, see fan reactions and take part without losing sight of which game everyone is talking about.

Share beyond the channel

Use the community feed to publish fan posts and respond to other supporters. Posts and comments make room for football conversation that is not limited to one fixture chat.

Predictions are your view of the match

When a fixture prediction is open, supporters can select the home team or the away team. Funspot records those selections so the community can see how opinion is distributed. A selection is a sporting prediction, not a guaranteed result, financial advice or a promise of a reward.

Once a match has a verified final result and the prediction is settled, the app can update the points record. An unsettled fixture should not be treated as a final prediction result. See the Terms of Use for the current rules.

Leaderboards show community progress

Leaderboards organize participating supporters by points recorded through eligible activity. They let fans compare prediction performance across the relevant community and time period. A position can change as more fixtures are completed and results are settled.

Leaderboard points are an in-app record. They should not be interpreted as cash, an investment balance or a guaranteed prize. The app's displayed rules govern how points are awarded and updated.
